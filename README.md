# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

Excel-based analysis of 138,116 e-commerce orders (2021–2025), covering sales, 
profitability, marketing performance, delivery, and returns.

## Tools
Excel (pivot tables, charts, dashboard design)

## Key Findings
1. **Consumer segment drives the business** — $97M of $177M total net sales — 
   but Business customers have the highest profit margin (46.0%) despite far 
   lower volume.
2. **Discounting looks policy-driven, not targeted** — rates stay flat around 
   18-19% across all marketing channels and 16-20% across all coupon codes.
3. **Customer lifetime value doesn't track with repeat purchases** — non-repeat 
   customers show ~$8,900 average CLV vs ~$1,300 for repeat customers, the 
   opposite of the usual assumption.
4. **Delivery speed affects satisfaction, but not in a straight line** — Early 
   rates highest (4.02), On Time next (3.73), Delayed lowest (3.22).
5. **Returns have no single dominant cause** — all 8 reasons sit within a 
   narrow 1,138-1,237 order range, meaning fixes need to be broad, not 
   targeted at one issue.
